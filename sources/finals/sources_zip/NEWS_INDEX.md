## 02. 2026-03-25 — President signs RA 12317 resetting the first BARMM parliamentary elections to Sept. 14, 2026

The law amends the Bangsamoro Organic Law to move the first regular parliamentary elections to the second Monday of September 2026, after the Supreme Court voided the earlier districting laws, and synchronizes later polls with national elections from 2031.

**Constitutional topics:** Autonomous regions (Art. X, Secs. 15-21); Congress's power to amend the organic act and set election dates; hold-over of the Bangsamoro Transition Authority

## 08. 2026-04-29 — Supreme Court announces Generillo v. Senate: "forthwith" means within a reasonable time

In a 14-0-1 decision dated April 22 and penned by Justice Zalameda, the Court dismissed as moot a mandamus petition to compel the Senate to convene at once as an impeachment court, while explaining that the Senate acted within a reasonable time in 2025.

**Constitutional topics:** Art. XI, Sec. 3(4) "trial by the Senate shall forthwith proceed"; mandamus against a co-equal branch; mootness and obiter
